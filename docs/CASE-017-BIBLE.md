# Case 017 — The Silence at Hollow Pines · writer's bible

**Spoilers. For the team, not for players.**

## The truth in one paragraph

Meera Rathore, born **Nisha Meera Kapoor**, is Dr. Anand Kapoor's younger sister and a former ICU
sister at Rathore Memorial Hospital, where she met Vikram in 2014. Brother and sister drained
₹1.84 crore from the Rathore Trust through a sham lab, Kapoor Diagnostics LLP, with Anand approving the
invoices and "Nisha M. Kapoor" as the second partner. Vikram found the payments and booked his lawyer
for Monday. Meera steamed open the lawyer's letter on Thursday and glued it shut. On Saturday Anand
drew potassium chloride at the hospital, handed Meera a filled syringe in the hall, did his usual
visit and **left at 21:40**. Meera came down two minutes later, told Vikram that Anand wanted a blood
sample, and injected him in the vein at the elbow. He fell at 21:44; she tapped "I'm OK" on his
watch. At 22:14 she phoned Anand from the study — the "voices" Sunita heard — then put Vikram in the
chair and burned the ampoule. Anand certified a natural death in the morning and fought the post-mortem.

## The three turns

1. **It wasn't natural, and it wasn't 1 AM** (round 1). The watch dies at 21:48.
2. **The doctor did it** (rounds 2–4). His "lie" about the time, the KCl in his name, the fraud. Everything
   points at Anand, and players are meant to believe it.
3. **The clock was wrong, and the hand was his sister's** (round 5). The gate recorder runs 18 minutes fast,
   so Anand told the truth and left before the fall. The only person in the house who could give an
   IV injection, who was awake and in the study, and who shared the motive, is Meera.

## Fair-play map

- Clock is wrong: inquest 08:05 vs camera 08:21; note 07:50 vs camera 07:56; recorder header "time source: manual" (round 2), confirmed in round 5.
- Meera is Anand's sister: same father in exhibits 10 and 33; "Bhaiya" number = "Anand (Dr.)" number in exhibits 15 and 32; wedding inscription 34; Kapoor's wife is Sunanda, so "Nisha" is not his wife (10, 24).
- Meera could do it: ICU sister certified in IV potassium (10, 33); "filled the organiser herself" (8).
- Meera lied: "asleep from 9.30" vs 22:14 call and study Wi-Fi (8, 32); "never opened it" vs re-glued flap (23, 27).
- Red herrings: Arjun (lies, but bar CCTV), Kabir (threat, but Dehradun tower), the will (executor: no change), Anand (fraud, KCl and apparent time lie — guilty of everything but the injection).
